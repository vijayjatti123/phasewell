Phasewell Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 October 2026

Phasewell is provided by VJ Softworks (Vijaykumar Jatti), Vijayapura, Karnataka, India. For privacy questions, contact vijaysjatti@gmail.com.

Information you choose to record

Phasewell processes information you enter on your Android device. This can include period dates, cycle preferences, pregnancy dates, symptoms, mood, flow, energy, sleep, medication notes, weight, water intake, sexual activity, custom symptoms, journal entries, ovulation-test results, cervical mucus, temperature measurements and their conditions, and visit-planning notes. The app uses these records to display your diary, estimates, trends, reports and optional reminders.

Local processing

The current Android app has no account system, developer-operated health-data server, advertising, analytics or automatic developer crash-reporting service. The Android app does not request Android's internet permission, so it cannot send your records over the internet. It does not send your health records to us. Core tracking operates offline. We do not sell your locally stored records or use them for advertising.

Storage and security

Android health records are encrypted using a key managed by Android Keystore. Reminder scheduling information and display preferences are stored separately and are not all covered by the health-record encryption. Optional app lock uses your device screen-lock authentication; Phasewell does not receive your device PIN or password. No security measure can protect against every form of device compromise or access.

Backups, reports and sharing

You can create a password-protected encrypted backup and choose its destination with Android's document picker. Choosing a cloud provider may upload the file to that provider. Keep the backup passphrase safe: we cannot recover it. Restoring a backup replaces local records after confirmation. If you turn on automatic backup, encrypted backups are saved about weekly to a folder you choose, or you receive a weekly reminder to save one. To do this, the backup passphrase is stored on your device encrypted with an Android Keystore key; it is removed when you turn automatic backup off or clear the app's data. The app keeps up to four of its own automatic backups in the chosen folder and does not touch other files there.

CSV reports, printed or saved PDF reports, and summaries shared through another app are not encrypted by Phasewell. Only export or share information with recipients and services you trust. Those services handle information under their own policies. Phasewell does not provide continuous partner access or cloud synchronization.

Permissions and external services

Notification permission is used for optional local reminders. Reboot events help restore reminder schedules. File operations use destinations you choose; Phasewell does not request access to all files. The app does not request contacts, location, microphone or camera access. It does not connect to Health Connect or automatically read wearable data.

Opening an external information link launches your browser. The destination website may receive information such as your IP address under its own policy. Your entered health records are not attached to these links by Phasewell. Android, Google Play, document providers and other apps may process information independently under their own policies.

Retention and deletion

Records remain locally until you delete them, clear app storage or uninstall the app. Use Settings > Delete all my data to remove the app's local records. Phasewell cannot delete copies you exported, sent to another person, or stored with a file or cloud provider; delete those separately. No remote recovery service is provided. Local deletion is not a guarantee of forensic erasure from a device or historic device snapshots.

Contacting support

If you email us, the information you choose to send is handled by our email provider and used to respond to your request. Avoid sending health records unless necessary. Support correspondence is retained while needed to resolve the request or meet applicable obligations; you may ask about deletion by contacting vijaysjatti@gmail.com. This support process is separate from the offline app.

Audience and health information

Phasewell is a personal health diary and is not designed as a service for children. No age information is sent to the developer by the app. Phasewell provides personal tracking and educational information. Its estimates do not establish safe dates for unprotected sex or diagnose a health condition.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app changes and will update its effective date. Material changes to data practices will be communicated through an appropriate notice. Contact vijaysjatti@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests.
